Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Tea + petroleum (Digboi is India's oldest refinery) — historic revenue streams.
  • Gateway to NE — strategic location for India's Act East policy.
  • Special-category state benefits — 90:10 funding ratio for centrally sponsored schemes.

Strains on record

  • Floods every year — Brahmaputra inundates ~⅓ of state, recurring fiscal drain.
  • Per-capita income below national average; industrial base limited.
  • FC15 cut share slightly — controversial given climate vulnerability.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Kamrup · structural

Kamrup

Guwahati (Kamrup Metro) is the GATEWAY TO NORTHEAST INDIA — the regional hub for industry, commerce, education and the primary airport for the NE's eight states. Its economy rests on tea (Assam grows >half of India's tea), oil (the Assam-Arakan basin holds ~¼ of India's oil reserves; the Guwahati Refinery is a central PSU), and a fast-growing service/hospitality sector. First NE district in this dataset.
