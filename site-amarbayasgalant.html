What happened to it

  1. 1937 CEStalinist religious purge (Mongolia)

    Soviet-backed communist state. Mongolia's 1930s purges destroyed most of its ~700 monasteries and killed thousands of monks; Amarbayasgalant was among the few partly spared.

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
