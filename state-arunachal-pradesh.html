Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest FC15 share boost (+0.39pp) — largest area, sparse population, huge forest weight.
  • Hydropower potential — 50,000+ MW theoretical capacity.
  • Strategic LAC frontier — central capital expenditure on roads, airports growing fast.

Strains on record

  • Lowest population density; service delivery extremely expensive per head.
  • Border with China is contested — security/diplomatic considerations limit civilian use of some land.
  • Connectivity to mainland still poor; many districts road-cut for months in monsoon.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
