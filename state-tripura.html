Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest tribal literacy in NE; strong public-services delivery for size.
  • Bamboo, rubber, agri-tech — niche but growing.
  • Special-category state benefits; 90:10 funding.

Strains on record

  • Landlocked + bordered by Bangladesh on three sides — connectivity bottleneck.
  • Heavy dependence on Center; own tax revenue under 3% of GSDP.
  • Small market — manufacturing scale economics don't work.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
