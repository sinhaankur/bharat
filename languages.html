12 place names

What the names mean

region

Kashmir

कश्मीर

Land desiccated from water. From Sanskrit Kaśmīra. The Nilamata Purana derives it from the sage Kashyapa (Kaśyapa) draining the vast lake Satisar — 'Kaśyapa-mira/mera', the land the sage reclaimed from water. Kalhana's Rajatarangini (12th c.) records the same tradition. A folk gloss reads 'ka' (water) + 'shimir' (to desiccate) = land dried out of water.

contested T2

country

Bharat

भारत

Land of the Bharatas / of Bharata. From the Bharata clan/king of the Rigveda — the Bharatas are a Rigvedic people, and 'Bhāratavarṣa' is the land of Bharata. Later tradition names it for Emperor Bharata (Rishabha's son in Jain accounts; Dushyanta & Shakuntala's son in the Mahabharata).

country

India

India

Land of the Sindhu (Indus) river. From the river Sindhu → Old Persian Hindu → Greek Indos → Latin India. The Greeks named the land beyond the Indus after the river.

T2

region

Hindustan

हिन्दुस्तान

Land of the Hindu (Indus) / of the Indus people. Persian: Hindū (from Sindhu) + -stān ('land'). Coined in the Persianate world for the land of the Indus and, later, the northern plains.

T2

city

Delhi

दिल्ली

Contested — 'Dhilli/Dhillika', or after King Dhilu. Traditionally from Raja Dhilu (c. 1st c. BCE); an Apabhramsha reading is 'dhili' (loose) for a loose iron pillar. The early name 'Dhillika' is recorded on a 12th-c. inscription. The site is old — Indraprastha of the Mahabharata is placed here.

contested T2

state

Maharashtra

महाराष्ट्र

The great realm / great nation. Sanskrit mahā ('great') + rāṣṭra ('nation, realm'). An alternate reading ties it to 'Maharashtri' Prakrit or to the Rathi/Ratta people (mahā-rathi, 'great charioteers').

contested T2

state

Karnataka

ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ

Lofty / black land ('karu' + 'nadu'). From Kannada karu ('lofty/black') + nāḍu ('land') — 'the lofty land' or 'land of black soil'. Recorded historically as 'Karnata desha'.

state

Tamil Nadu

தமிழ்நாடு

Land of the Tamils / Tamil-speaking land. Tamil ('the Tamil language/people') + nāḍu ('land, country'). 'Tamilakam' — the ancient Tamil country — is named in Sangam literature.

T2

state

Kerala

കേരളം

Contested — 'land of coconuts' or 'Chera-alam'. Popular gloss: keram ('coconut') + alam ('land'). Scholarly reading: from the Chera dynasty — 'Cheralam' → Kerala. The Ashokan Edicts (3rd c. BCE) name 'Keralaputra' (son of the Cheras).

contested T1

region

Punjab

ਪੰਜਾਬ

Five waters (land of the five rivers). Persian panj ('five') + āb ('water') — the land of the five rivers (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej). The ancient Sanskrit name was 'Sapta Sindhu' (seven rivers).

region

Bengal

বঙ্গ

From 'Vanga' — an ancient janapada. From Vaṅga, an ancient kingdom/janapada of the eastern Ganges delta; 'Vangala' → Bengal. Possibly from a Dravidian/Austroasiatic root or the Bang tribe.

contested T2

state

Gujarat

ગુજરાત

Land of the Gurjaras. From 'Gurjara-rāṣṭra' — the land of the Gurjara people, who established power here in the early medieval period.
