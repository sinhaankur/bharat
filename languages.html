India speaks languages from unrelated families, written in scripts that mostly share one ancient ancestor — Brahmi, carved on Ashoka's pillars. This hub pulls the whole thread together: the family trees, the Brahmi-descended scripts, self-hosted fonts for every hand, source texts across languages, and how the places got their names. How the places got their names

Most of India's names are ancient — first written down in the centuries BCE or the early CE. Each card: what the name means, where it came from, and the earliest text that records it. Where scholars differ we say so and cite the reading rather than pick a winner — sourced, or it's a gap.
