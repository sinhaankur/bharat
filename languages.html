Languages of Bharat · the hub

Every language, every script — one place

India speaks languages from unrelated families written in scripts that mostly share one ancestor. This hub pulls the whole thread together: the family trees, the Brahmi-descended scripts and the Bharati link-script, self-hosted fonts for every script, and source texts opened across languages with attributed translations. Everything holds to one rule — real, sourced translations only; honest gaps everywhere else.
