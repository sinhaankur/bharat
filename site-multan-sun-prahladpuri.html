Multan's celebrated Sun Temple was one of the most famous shrines of the ancient subcontinent, its wealth described by Arab and Greek writers. Destroyed after the Muslim conquest of Multan; the adjacent Prahladpuri temple was reduced over centuries and the last structure was lost in 1992.

    Ghaznavid / successive rulers. The Multan Sun Temple, hugely wealthy, was destroyed after the Muslim conquest; the Prahladpuri temple was damaged repeatedly and its final structure collapsed amid the 1992 communal violence.
