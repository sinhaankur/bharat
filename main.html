Vedic and Mauryan India

C. 1500 TO 500 BCE

यज्ञ

“He verily knows it, or perhaps he knows not.”

The Hymn of Creation ends in open doubt about how everything began.

SOURCEDRigveda 10.129.7, trans. R. T. H. Griffith, 1896

One text, many tongues
C. 320 TO 185 BCE

धम्म

“This is the repentance of Beloved-of-the-Gods.”

After Kalinga, Ashoka published his own regret. Edict find-spots run from Kandahar to Karnataka, in Brahmi, Kharoshthi, Greek and Aramaic.

SOURCEDMajor Rock Edict 13, trans. E. Hultzsch, 1925
