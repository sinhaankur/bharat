Ashoka's rule of the land

How far did Mauryan power reach, and what did it stand for? We can answer both from Ashoka's own edicts — carved across the empire around 268–232 BCE and unreadable for two millennia until Brahmi was deciphered in 1837. Each edict is a fixed point where his authority could cut stone, so the map of find-spots is the footprint of the empire; the texts state its ethics.
