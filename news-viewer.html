Read any story, with its signals

Open a news item in a frame and read it next to two things: where third parties place the outlet, and transparent, per-article signals you can check yourself.

The atlas records; it does not judge. The outlet lean is a third-party assessment carried as-is. The per-article signals are a plain checklist — each one shows the rule that fired. Neither is a verdict on whether a story is true.

What the signals mean. lean is the outlet's position per outside aggregations (left / centre / right), recorded with its source. signal is a per-article pattern: a bait-style headline, no byline in the link, a paywall, ALL-CAPS, or number-bait. A signal is a prompt to read carefully, not proof of anything. Every item here is unverified until a person approves it — the frame is the original page, served by its publisher.
