Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Steel + coal — 20% of India's steel production; mineral royalties cushion finances.
  • Forest cover ~44% — eligible for FC's forest-weight component.
  • FC15 share rose ~0.3pp — recognising forest and demographic-need parameters.

Strains on record

  • Maoist insurgency in south Bastar persists — security costs depress development spending.
  • Heavy mining + thermal dependence — environmental and just-transition risk.
  • Per-capita income below national average; tribal population (~30%) historically marginalised.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
