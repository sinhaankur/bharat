The Ajanta chaitya hall

A Buddhist prayer hall carved into the living rock — its horseshoe vault, colonnade of pillars, and the votive stupa at the far apse, shown not as the weathered cave of today but painted and vivid, as pilgrims once saw it by lamplight.
