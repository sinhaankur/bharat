Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Resource-rich: 28% of India's coal, large iron ore + uranium reserves.
  • Strategic location — supplies raw material to steel belts in Odisha/WB/Chhattisgarh.
  • Forest cover ~30% gets weight in FC formula.

Strains on record

  • Per-capita income among lowest in India; mineral wealth doesn't trickle to local population.
  • Tribal land rights vs mining lease conflicts unresolved — recurring agitations.
  • Tax administration weak; informal economy dominates.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Purba Singhbhum · structural dysfunction

Purba Singhbhum

Jamshedpur is the only major city in India with NO democratically elected municipality. It was run as a private Tata township via the Jamshedpur Notified Area Committee (JNAC), services delivered by JUSCO (a Tata Steel subsidiary). A 1993 constitutional provision for an 'industrial township' (Art. 243Q proviso) let it avoid a municipal corporation despite the 74th Amendment. So 'who is responsible for civic money' has never had a normal municipal answer here.

source T3

Purba Singhbhum · governance capture

Purba Singhbhum

In 2025 the Jharkhand govt dissolved JNAC and formed the Jamshedpur Industrial Area Committee — a NOMINATED (not elected) body of 27: 6 state govt, 11 Tata Steel, 10 local. The East Singhbhum Deputy Commissioner is vice-chairperson; the district-in-charge minister chairs. MLA Saryu Roy called it unconstitutional for sidestepping the 74th Amendment's elected-local-body mandate. Tata holds the largest single bloc on the body governing the city.
