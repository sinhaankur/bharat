West Bengal's DA arrears: 11 years of delayed pay, settled by the Supreme Court

State employees' dearness allowance fell years behind central rates; after a 2022 Calcutta HC order the Supreme Court finally ruled (5 Feb 2026) that WB must clear ~11 years of DA arrears (2009–2019, ~₹10,000 cr under the 5th Pay Commission) to ~20 lakh employees.

The promiseDearness Allowance is a legally enforceable component of a government employee's pay, payable to compensate for inflation. Supreme Court orders West Bengal to pay 11 years of DA arrears to employees T4
The resultDA disbursal lagged central rates for years; the Supreme Court's 5 Feb 2026 judgment upheld the employees' entitlement and directed WB to clear arrears for 2009–2019, with a court-monitored committee (chaired by Justice Indu Malhotra) fixing the instalment schedule.
Actual costApprox. DA arrears for 2009–2019 under the 5th Pay Commission, as reported around the Supreme Court proceedings. A court-appointed committee is fixing the exact figure and schedule. DA outstanding of ₹10,000 crore to be cleared under 5th Pay Commission: SC orders T4
Human impactAbout 20 lakh West Bengal government employees went years without DA at central rates — on the order of ₹50,000 of delayed pay each — until the Supreme Court's Feb 2026 judgment made the arrears legally due and set up a monitored payout.
  1. 2022-05-20 · audit_flagCalcutta High Court orders WB to pay DA at central rates

    The Calcutta High Court ruled that West Bengal must align its dearness-allowance disbursal with central government rates, treating DA as an enforceable entitlement of state employees. The state challenged the order in the Supreme Court.

    The Supreme Court directed West Bengal to pay 25% of the outstanding DA arrears (accumulated 2009–2019 under the 5th Pay Commission, ~₹10,000 cr) to its employees within six weeks — an interim step while the balance was worked out.

    DA outstanding of ₹10,000 crore to be cleared under 5th Pay Commission: SC orders T4 reported no matching news report found yet
  3. 2026-02-05 · actionSupreme Court final judgment: WB must clear ~11 years of DA arrears

    On 5 Feb 2026 the Supreme Court upheld employees' entitlement and directed West Bengal to clear DA arrears for 2009–2019 for ~20 lakh employees, with a court-appointed committee (chaired by former Justice Indu Malhotra, with the CAG or a nominee) fixing the exact amount and instalment schedule. Amounts paid are not to be recovered even if the law later changes.

Birbhum's MGNREGS money: frozen at the top for four years

Centre froze WB's MGNREGS funds under Sec 27 (Mar 2022); ₹3,038 cr dues piled up; resumed Jun 2026 after Supreme Court intervention. Dysfunction = money WITHHELD upstream, not misspent.

The promiseMGNREGA guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adults volunteer to do unskilled manual work. The Mahatma Gandhi NREGA, 2005 — Section 3 T1
The resultFund release to West Bengal was frozen from 9 Mar 2022 under Sec 27; no MGNREGS wages flowed to WB districts for ~4 years until a June 2026 resumption after Supreme Court intervention.
Actual costCentre-stated pending MGNREGS dues to WB (Rajya Sabha, 2025) — the withheld amount, not district-split. Centre owes ₹3,000 crore MGNREGA dues to Bengal (Rajya Sabha) T4
Human impactThe statutory job guarantee stopped functioning in WB's rural districts: households that would have earned MGNREGS wages did not, unbuilt assets and reported distress migration followed. ~₹333 of guaranteed-wage money per resident sat withheld upstream.
  1. 2022-03-09 · delayCentre freezes MGNREGS fund release to West Bengal (Sec 27)

    The Union stopped MGNREGS fund release to West Bengal from 9 March 2022 under Section 27 of the MGNREGA Act, citing non-compliance/corruption. Every MGNREGS-dependent district, including Birbhum, was cut off from wages at source.

    Centre owes ₹3,000 crore MGNREGA dues to Bengal (Rajya Sabha) T4 reported no matching news report found yet
  3. 2026-05-01 · actionSupreme Court intervention pushes MGNREGS restart

Ludhiana, Punjab · open

Punjab's pension arrears: age-staggered payout struck down, full release ordered

Punjab tried to pay pension arrears in age-based instalments; the Punjab & Haryana High Court quashed that plan as arbitrary (Article 14) and ordered pending DA/DR released to ~4 lakh pensioners (and ~3.5 lakh employees) at central rates by 30 Jun 2026 — implementation reportedly still pending.

The promisePunjab accepted payment of DA/DR to its pensioners and employees on the Central Government pattern (per the 6th Punjab Pay Commission). Punjab & Haryana High Court quashes staggered pension arrears plan, directs Punjab to release pending DA/DR T4
The resultPunjab delayed the DA/DR citing financial constraints and issued a Feb 2025 plan staggering arrears by age. The High Court struck that down as arbitrary and violative of Article 14, and directed release to all employees & pensioners at the All-India-Service rate by 30 Jun 2026, with the Chief Secretary to report compliance. Reports say implementation remained pending.
Actual costNo single clean sourced rupee total for the Punjab pension arrears — left an explicit gap. The DOCUMENTED scale is the headcount: ~4 lakh pensioners (and ~3.5 lakh employees), ~7.5 lakh people awaiting their legal dues. Punjab pending DA dispute: 7.5 lakh employees and pensioners waiting for their legal due T4
Human impactAbout 4 lakh Punjab pensioners (with ~3.5 lakh serving employees, ~7.5 lakh in all) were kept waiting for DA/DR they were legally owed — while All-India-Service officers drew full benefits from the same Consolidated Fund, a disparity the High Court flagged. The exact rupee arrears per pensioner is a gap; the documented fact is the delay and its scale.
  1. 2025-02-18 · delayPunjab issues a plan to pay pension arrears in age-based instalments

    Citing financial constraints, Punjab issued a 'Liquidation Plan' dated 18 Feb 2025 that staggered payment of pension DA/DR arrears based on the pensioner's age — deferring dues the state had accepted on the Central Government pattern.

    Punjab & Haryana High Court quashes staggered pension arrears plan T4 reported no matching news report found yet
  2. 2026-02-01 · actionHigh Court quashes the age-staggered plan, orders full release

    The Punjab & Haryana High Court struck down the age-based staggering as arbitrary and violative of Article 14, directing the state and its corporations to release all pending DA/DR to employees and pensioners at the All-India-Service (central) rate by 30 Jun 2026, with the Chief Secretary to file a compliance report.

    Punjab & Haryana High Court directs Punjab to release pending DA/DR T4 documented no matching news report found yet
  3. 2026-06-30 · shortfallDeadline arrives; implementation reportedly still pending

    As of the court-set 30 Jun 2026 deadline, reporting indicated Punjab had yet to implement the revised DA/DR for its ~3.5 lakh employees and ~4 lakh pensioners, again citing fiscal constraints — leaving ~7.5 lakh people awaiting their legally-affirmed dues.

    Punjab pending DA dispute: 7.5 lakh employees and pensioners waiting for their legal due T4 reported no matching news report found yet
