The 'earliest public water tank of the ancient world' (c. 2500 BCE), 12×7 m and 2.4 m deep, watertight with bitumen — widely read as a ritual bathing structure, a possible root of later Dharmic purification practice. Mohenjo-daro (pop. ~40,000) was abandoned c. 1700 BCE. NOTE: attributing a religion to the Indus Valley is speculative — the script is undeciphered and there are no confirmed temples; this is included as a proto-historic SACRED/ritual site, honestly labelled.
