Recorded damage

What happened to it

  1. 1193 CEBakhtiyar Khilji

    Ghurid / Delhi Sultanate general. Persian chronicle Tabaqat-i-Nasiri records the sack of the monastic complex at Bihar; tradition holds the library burned for months and monks were killed. Modern scholars debate whether Nalanda specifically or Odantapuri was the primary target, but the destruction of Bihar's viharas c. 1193 is well attested.

    estimate Chronicles describe monks slain; numbers not quantified reliably.

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
