A Kushan-era monastery in the Zabarwan hills, identified by Aurel Stein as Sadarhadvana, home of the Bodhisattva Nagarjuna. Traditionally the site of the Fourth Buddhist Council under Kanishka I — pivotal for Mahayana Buddhism. Excavated 1923; its terracotta tiles depict Central-Asian faces (Yarkand/Kashgar), the 'only remains of their kind'. ASI-protected ruin.
