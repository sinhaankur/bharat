GRAMMAR

Design idea: extreme restraint — one ink, one ground. Triangle-torso figures = a tiny icon grammar. Proof that a two-token palette can carry an entire world.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: women painted white rice-paste figures onto red-ochre mud walls for weddings and harvest — among India's oldest unbroken wall traditions. After: moved from wall to paper and canvas in the 1970s (Jivya Soma Mashe), now a GI-tagged global style.
