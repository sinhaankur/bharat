pila maati — yellow earth #c9962f · PATTERN 2.1:1 on ground · decor only

GRAMMAR

Design idea: a fixed palette of four or five soils, nothing else. Order carries meaning: red first for ancestors and fertility, then black for Shiva, then white for food.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: women paint mud walls for the harvest and wedding seasons, using only the soils that occur locally; the craft passes from mother to daughter. A white mud coat goes on first, then red, black, yellow and white are laid on while it is still wet. After: on 12 May 2020 Sohrai-Khovar became the first Geographical Indication from Jharkhand.
