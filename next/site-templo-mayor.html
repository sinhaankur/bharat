The great double pyramid at the heart of the Aztec capital Tenochtitlan. Razed by the Spanish after 1521; a cathedral and colonial city were built directly over it. Rediscovered under Mexico City in 1978. The clearest case of a temple erased and overbuilt by a colonial-Christian power.

What happened to it

  1. 1521 CEHernán Cortés & the Spanish conquest

    Spanish Empire / Catholic conquest. After the fall of Tenochtitlan the temple was demolished and Mexico City's cathedral raised over the sacred precinct; the Mexica religion was suppressed.

    estimate The conquest of the Americas caused catastrophic indigenous death (war + disease); White's chart puts the toll at ~15 million.

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
