Limits

What this project does not claim

It is independent and non-commercial, and is not affiliated with any government body. Records about office-holders describe the office and what cited documents say; they are not accusations. Lean labels for news outlets record published third-party assessments and are not this project's verdict. Encyclopedia entries are used to find facts, not to rest on them.
