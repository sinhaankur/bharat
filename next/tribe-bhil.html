GRAMMAR

Design idea: fields built entirely from dots — a pointillist fill token. The dots carry colour and texture at once; the figure is the negative space between them.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: the Bhil — one of India's largest Adivasi nations — marked walls with pithora ritual horses and deities for vows and harvest. After: Lado Bai, Bhuri Bai and others brought it to canvas from the 1980s Bharat Bhavan circle.
