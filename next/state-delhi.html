Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest per-capita income among states/UTs (~₹4.6 lakh).
  • Service-economy + central-government footprint generate huge tax base.
  • Strong public-services investment (mohalla clinics, public schools) per capita.

Strains on record

  • As UT, gets no Finance Commission devolution — depends on central grants & own revenue.
  • Center-state friction (UT vs state powers) tied up in Supreme Court for years.
  • Air pollution + groundwater depletion — environmental costs unrecouped.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
