The Dongria Kondh worship Niyam Raja, who is believed to dwell at Niyam Dongar, the Mountain of the Law. They call themselves Jharnia, 'protectors of the streams'. In 2013, in a Supreme-Court-mandated vote, all 12 village councils rejected Vedanta's bauxite mine — a rare victory for an indigenous people defending a sacred site against industry. Included because native sacred sites are usually erased, not mapped.
