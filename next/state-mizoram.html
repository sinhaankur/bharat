Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest literacy in NE (~91%); strong public health.
  • Bordering Myanmar + Bangladesh — Act East and Kaladan project corridor.
  • Cleanest state by several rankings — low pollution baseline.

Strains on record

  • Tiny tax base; nearly all expenditure financed by Center.
  • Mountainous terrain — limited arable land; food largely imported.
  • Recent Myanmar refugee inflows strain local services and fiscal capacity.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
