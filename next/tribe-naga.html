GRAMMAR

Design idea: bands as credentials. Who may wear which band is the grammar, so the system’s tokens carry rank, not just colour.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: every Naga tribe has its own shawls, and designs are specific to gender and age. Red is for men; a young woman’s shawl must not be red. The Elicüra, the Chakhesang “Feasts of Merit” shawl, could be worn only by those who had earned it. After: Chakhesang shawls received a Geographical Indication in 2017, Nagaland’s third.
