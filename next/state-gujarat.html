Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Ease-of-business leader: cluster manufacturing (chemicals, pharma, gems), GIFT City IFSC.
  • Per-capita income among top 5 — diversified economy beyond just Surat/Ahmedabad.
  • Lowest fiscal deficit consistently — Gujarat model of conservative state finances.

Strains on record

  • Net donor by a wide margin; refining and chemical exports route central taxes via Mumbai/Delhi attribution.
  • Human development indices lag economic numbers — child malnutrition, female labour participation weak.
  • Vulnerable to oil shocks and global trade disruptions given export dependence.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Surat · structural

Surat

Surat is the world's diamond-cutting capital — ~90% of the world's natural diamonds and ~25% of lab-grown diamonds are polished here, across ~6,000 units (70% MSMEs). It's also 'Manchester of India': ~65,000 textile factories, ~40% of national man-made fabric, ~90% of synthetic fabric. An MSME-driven export economy uniquely exposed to global shocks (e.g. demonetisation hit it hard).

Built where water returns

Ahmadabad · 2025

Sabarmati river / floodplain + city lakes

Sabarmati Riverfront reclaimed floodplain and channelised the river; critics (who took Gujarat civic bodies to NGT) link narrowing + blocked drains + lost lakes (~2.2% blue cover) to worsening urban flooding; lower promenade submerged 2025.

Vadodara · 2021

Vishwamitri river

Paryavaran Suraksha Samiti took VMC to NGT (2016) over choked drains and floodplain encroachment; per the petitioner VMC did not act on the 2021 NGT directive. Mega-highways/bullet-train alignments also cited as obstructing the natural river system.
