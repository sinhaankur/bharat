GRAMMAR

Design idea: a procession grid. Horses move left to right inside a rectangular boundary that maps the Rathwa homeland; every god, animal and plant has its own colour, so colour is a key, not decoration.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: a vow painting for Baba Pithora on the house wall, made by painters called Lakhara; the walls are plastered with cow dung for seven days first, and pigments are mixed with milk and mahua liquor. After: paper, fabric and poster colours since recent decades; the wall painting still marks the ceremony.
