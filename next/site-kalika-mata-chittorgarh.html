An 8th-century Sun temple inside Chittor Fort, damaged during the sieges of Chittorgarh (notably Alauddin Khilji's 1303 siege and Akbar's 1568 siege, in which the jauhar and massacres are recorded). Rebuilt and rededicated to Kali. UNESCO World Heritage (Hill Forts of Rajasthan).
