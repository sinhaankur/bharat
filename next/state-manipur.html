Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Strategic — borders Myanmar, gateway for Act East trade.
  • Sericulture, organic farming, sports excellence (boxing, weightlifting).
  • FC15 share rose — recognising NE special needs.

Strains on record

  • Ethnic conflict (Meitei-Kuki crisis since May 2023) has devastated economy and fiscal flows.
  • Heavy dependence on Center; own revenue under 2% of GSDP.
  • Insurgency-related shutdowns periodically halt economic activity.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
