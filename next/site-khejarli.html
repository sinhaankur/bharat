In 1730, when the Maharaja of Jodhpur's men came to fell khejri trees, Amrita Devi and then 363 Bishnois were killed hugging the trees rather than let them fall — the earliest recorded environmental martyrdom, and the root of the later Chipko movement. A living tradition where protecting nature IS the religion.
