The oldest known temple on Earth — monumental T-shaped pillars raised by hunter-gatherers ~9600 BCE, 6,000 years before Stonehenge and 7,000 before the pyramids. It rewrote the story of religion: temples may have come BEFORE farming and cities. Only ~5% excavated. UNESCO World Heritage.
