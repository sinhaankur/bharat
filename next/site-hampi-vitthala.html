Vitthala Temple, Hampi (Vijayanagara)

What happened to it

  1. 1565 CEDeccan Sultanate confederacy

    Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Golconda, Bidar sultanates. After defeating Vijayanagara at Talikota, the victorious armies looted and burned the capital over months, one of the largest such destructions of a medieval Indian city.
