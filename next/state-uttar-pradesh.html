Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Largest FC horizontal share — gets ~₹2 lakh crore/year of central tax devolution.
  • Demographic weight: 17% of India's population gives it political and fiscal heft.
  • Recent infrastructure push (expressways, Jewar airport, defence corridor) starting to show results.

Strains on record

  • Net recipient by far — devolution + grants exceed own tax revenue most years.
  • Lowest per-capita GSDP among large states; ~half the national average.
  • Tax administration capacity weak; large informal sector limits own-revenue mobilisation.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Lucknow · structural

Lucknow

Lucknow Municipal Corporation's 2025-26 budget jumped ~50% to ₹4,304.53 cr (from ₹2,865 cr) — but its own tax/fee revenue is only ₹295 cr, i.e. the civic budget is overwhelmingly grant/transfer-funded, not locally raised. A heavy grant-dependence typical of north-Indian ULBs, contrasting with Kochi's healthier own-source share. ~₹600 cr earmarked for sanitation after poor Swachh rankings.
