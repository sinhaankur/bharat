Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Granary of India — 22% of national wheat, 11% of rice; food-security backbone.
  • Per-capita income above national average; strong rural Punjabi economy plus diaspora.
  • FC15 share rose moderately — partial recognition of stagnation.

Strains on record

  • Highest debt-to-GSDP ratio among large states (~47%) — fiscal stress is structural.
  • Free electricity to agriculture has wrecked groundwater; ecological + fiscal crisis.
  • Industrial economy stagnant since 1990s — Ludhiana hosiery cluster losing ground to Tirupur.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Ludhiana · structural

Ludhiana

Ludhiana is the 'Manchester of India' for a different reason than Surat — a diversified MSME manufacturing powerhouse: >1.5 lakh registered MSMEs, ~50% of India's bicycles, dominant in woollen hosiery/knitwear, plus auto-parts, hand tools, machine tools. A multi-cluster, risk-diversified industrial economy built bottom-up — Punjab's largest city & industrial hub.
