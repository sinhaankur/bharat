Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Largest GSDP — ~₹38 lakh crore in FY24, ~14% of national output.
  • Mumbai = financial capital: RBI, SEBI, BSE/NSE, all big banks HQ'd here.
  • Highest own-tax mobilisation in absolute terms; least dependent on Center for revenue.

Strains on record

  • Severe net donor — pays ~₹4 to the Center for every ₹1 it gets back as devolution.
  • Sharp intra-state inequality: Mumbai-Pune corridor pulls in capital, Vidarbha/Marathwada stagnate.
  • Urban primacy comes with brittle infrastructure — Mumbai monsoon flooding, suburban rail at 250% capacity.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Greater Bombay · structural

Greater Bombay

BMC (Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation) is India's richest civic body. Its 2025-26 budget of ₹74,427 cr is the largest in its history — bigger than the annual budget of several Indian STATES, and ~25× Kolkata's KMC. Over a decade BMC allocated ₹2.19 lakh cr. The 'money flowing through one district' here dwarfs most state-level flows.

source T1

Greater Bombay · governance deficit

Greater Bombay

Like Kolkata, Mumbai's civic governance has run WITHOUT an elected body since March 2022 — the ₹74,427 cr budget was presented to a state-appointed administrator (BMC Commissioner Bhushan Gagrani), not an elected council. Elections were finally held in Jan 2026. A democratic-deficit dysfunction in the country's richest municipality.

source T3

Mula-Mutha river
