One of the great Buddhist monastic centres of Gandhara near Jalalabad — ~23,000 Greco-Buddhist sculptures were unearthed here, the finest fusion of Greek and Indian art. Almost entirely destroyed in the Afghan civil war. A staggering loss of shared Indo-Hellenic Buddhist heritage.
