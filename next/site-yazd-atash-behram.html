Enshrines a sacred fire burning since ~470 CE — the highest-grade Zoroastrian fire in Iran. Zoroastrianism, the ancient faith of pre-Islamic Persia, shaped ideas across many later religions; its followers are now a tiny, endangered minority in their homeland. A native faith that predates the Abrahamic religions.
