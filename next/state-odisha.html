Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Mineral-rich (iron ore, bauxite, coal) — royalty receipts steady; aluminium hub.
  • Disaster-management capacity built post-1999 cyclone is best-in-class globally.
  • Strong fiscal discipline under one-party rule for 24 years (until 2024).

Strains on record

  • Resource-curse risk — mining receipts can mask weak diversification.
  • Per-capita income below national average despite resource wealth.
  • Coastal vulnerability — Bay of Bengal cyclones intensifying with climate change.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
