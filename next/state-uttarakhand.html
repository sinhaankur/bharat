Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest per-capita income among hill states; pharma/auto belt around Haridwar-Rudrapur.
  • Hydropower potential + tourism (Char Dham, Jim Corbett, Rishikesh).
  • Strong literacy, gender outcomes — small population, manageable governance unit.

Strains on record

  • Ecologically fragile — Himalayan disasters (2013 Kedarnath, 2021 Chamoli, 2023 Joshimath) recurring.
  • Out-migration from hill districts — 'ghost villages' phenomenon, agriculture abandoned.
  • Heavily dependent on tourism + centrally funded hydro projects.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
