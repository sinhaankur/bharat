At Aror (Roruka) — capital of the Sauvira kingdom and of Hindu Sindh under Raja Dahir until the Arab conquest (712 CE). A natural-cave Devi temple, among the holiest Hindu sites in Pakistan, still visited. Roruka is also named in early Buddhist literature — a genuinely multi-faith ancient city.
