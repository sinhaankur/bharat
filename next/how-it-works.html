How it works

The project in plain terms.

The method

Four rules that hold everywhere

Rule one

Sourced, or it is a gap

Every figure cites a source or is shown as a declared gap. Nothing is estimated to fill a hole.

Rule two

Fact and framing stay apart

What the record shows sits beside commentary, each labelled, never blended.

Rule three

One nav, one name

Every page has one name and one home, drawn from a single list.

Rule four

Open, or keyed

Open data ships. Anything paid or live is opt-in with your own key, kept in your browser.
