Beyond the famous Martand and Avantipura ruins, Kashmir's smaller village and town temples were destroyed en masse during the reign of Sultan Sikandar 'Butshikan' (1389–1413), recorded in Jonaraja's continuation of the Rajatarangini and in Pandit chronicles. This entry marks the district-wide loss of small shrines that individually left no ruin — the 'sourced-or-gap' way to show a destruction that erased its own evidence.

What happened to it

  1. 1400 CESultan Sikandar Shah Miri ('Butshikan')

    Sultan of Kashmir. Kashmiri chronicles record a systematic campaign against the Valley's temples and a wave of forced conversion during Sikandar's reign; his minister Suha Bhatta (a convert) is named as an enforcer.

    estimate Chronicles describe destruction of shrines across the Valley and pressure that reduced the Pandit population — figures are chronicle estimates, not censused.

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
