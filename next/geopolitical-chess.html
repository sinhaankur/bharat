The moves

WhenMoveWhat it didKind
1971Nixon closes the gold windowThe US ends dollar-gold convertibility, floating the world onto a pure fiat dollar standard — the board as we know it is set.framing
1974The petrodollar handshakeSaudi Arabia agrees to price oil in dollars in exchange for US security — locking oil demand to the dollar for a generation.framing
2015–18CIPS + Belt and RoadChina launches its own cross-border payment system (CIPS) and lends across Asia and Africa — the first bricks of a parallel board.framing
2022Russia cut off from SWIFTAfter the invasion of Ukraine, the US and allies freeze Russian reserves and cut major banks off dollar clearing — the board weaponised in the open.record
2022–24Rupee oil, UPI goes abroadIndia buys discounted Russian crude settled partly in rupees/dirhams and extends UPI cross-border — routing around the dollar at the edges.record
2023Lula's local-currency pushBrazil and China agree to settle bilateral trade in their own currencies, and BRICS talks up de-dollarisation — chipping at the board's edge.framing
2023–24Munitions for MoscowNorth Korea ships artillery and missiles to Russia in exchange for aid and technology — two off-board pieces reinforcing each other.framing
2024Advanced-chip export controls tightenThe US widens curbs on selling leading-edge chips and tools to China — moving the fight from money to technology.record
2025Tariff walls go back upA more protectionist Washington leans on broad tariffs and tighter tech controls — allies and rivals alike are pushed to hedge, and the incentive to hold assets outside the dollar rises.framing
2025Yuan oil & CIPS keep wideningMore Gulf and Russian energy is invoiced or settled in yuan, and CIPS membership grows — the parallel board gains users, though the dollar still clears the vast majority.framing
2025Trade fully redirected eastWith Europe closed, the bulk of Russian oil now flows to India and China, settled in rupees, yuan and dirhams via Gulf hubs — the clearest running test of living off the dollar board.framing
2026Local-currency push, no common coinBRICS+ keeps expanding local-currency settlement and gold holdings, but India–China rivalry again blocks any shared currency — fragmentation at the edges, not a rival board.framing
2026Rupee rails go cross-borderIndia widens INR trade settlement and UPI links (UAE, Singapore and more) while still buying discounted Russian crude — deepening its un-cornered swing position.framing
2026Hedging both boards openlyRiyadh keeps dollar oil pricing and US security ties while courting yuan settlement and BRICS — maximising leverage by refusing to choose.framing
2026The silicon shield under strainUS, Japanese and European fabs slowly ramp while cross-strait pressure stays high — the chip chokehold is still Taiwan's, but the board is racing to copy the key square.framing
