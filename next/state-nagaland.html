Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest tribal literacy + English fluency among NE states.
  • Cultural tourism (Hornbill Festival) — niche but visible.
  • FC15 share rose — recognising small population's per-capita needs.

Strains on record

  • Lowest own-tax/GSDP ratio in India (under 1.5%).
  • Insurgency + Naga Accord pending for decades — political uncertainty.
  • Limited industrial base; bamboo + niche agriculture only.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
