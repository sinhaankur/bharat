Rajput

sandstone, mirror and miniature

the Rajput register of the Indic design systems. Heritage (medieval Rajasthan courts, c. 8th–18th c.): - Fort & haveli sandstone — Jaisalmer "Golden City" yellow sandstone; Jodhpur. - Sheesh Mahal (mirror-work) — tiny mirrors on indigo/emerald ground. - Rajput / Mewar miniature painting — vivid indigo, vermilion, malachite green, and gold leaf on cream wasli paper.
