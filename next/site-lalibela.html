Eleven churches carved top-down from solid rock in the 12th–13th c. to recreate Jerusalem in Ethiopia — the Church of St George (Bete Giyorgis) is the most famous. A living centre of Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity, one of the world's oldest churches. UNESCO World Heritage.
