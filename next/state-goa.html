Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Highest per-capita income in India (~₹4.5 lakh in FY24).
  • Tourism + mining + IT services + pharma — diversified for size.
  • Small population means per-capita central spend is high.

Strains on record

  • Mining ban + COVID hit tourism — twin shocks exposed economic narrowness.
  • Net donor relative to own-tax capacity; but tiny in absolute terms.
  • Environmental degradation — coastal erosion, illegal mining residue, over-tourism.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
