The most sacred temple of the Inca, its walls once covered in gold. After the conquest the Spanish stripped the gold and built the Santo Domingo convent directly on top of the Inca stonework — which still stands, and survived earthquakes that toppled the colonial church above it. A literal layering of colonial power over a native temple.

  1. 1533 CEFrancisco Pizarro & the Spanish conquest

    Spanish Empire / Catholic conquest. The Inca sun temple was looted of its gold and a Dominican convent built over it; Inca religion was suppressed across the Andes.
