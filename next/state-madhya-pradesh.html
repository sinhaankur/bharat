Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Third-largest FC horizontal share — strong recognition of size and need.
  • Soybean, wheat, pulses heartland — agricultural GSDP one of highest in India.
  • FC15 share rose — recognising forest cover (extra weight) and demographic needs.

Strains on record

  • Per-capita income well below national average; large rural+tribal population.
  • Manufacturing growth slow — Indore is bright spot but state-wide industrial base thin.
  • Net recipient — devolution exceeds own tax revenue nearly every year.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
