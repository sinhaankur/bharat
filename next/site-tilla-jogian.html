The highest peak of the eastern Salt Range — a Hindu yogi (Nath) monastic complex ~2,000 years old, where Guru Nanak also meditated 40 days. Temples destroyed by Ahmad Shah Abdali's 18th-c. raids, rebuilt by Ranjit Singh, abandoned after 1947. A shared Hindu–Sikh–yogi sacred summit now at risk of vanishing.
