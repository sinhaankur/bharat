GRAMMAR

Design idea: every surface fills with a signature of dots and dashes — a texture token. A vivid multi-hue palette, yet unified by that single fill rhythm.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: digna and bhittichitra — ritual mud-wall and floor art of the Pardhan Gond, in pigment from charcoal, soil, sap and dung. After: Jangarh Singh Shyam turned it into studio painting in 1980s Bhopal; 'Jangarh Kalam' is now a named school.
