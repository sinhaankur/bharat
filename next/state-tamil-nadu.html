Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Most industrialised state — auto, leather, textiles, electronics manufacturing clusters.
  • Highest own-tax/GSDP ratio among large states (~7.5%); strong VAT/GST collection machinery.
  • Best public services delivery: health, mid-day meals, women's literacy lead national averages.

Strains on record

  • Net donor: contributes ~3× what it receives as devolution.
  • FC15 horizontal share barely moved despite stronger fiscal effort — penalises performance.
  • Water-stressed; Chennai's 2019 'Day Zero' showed urban planning hasn't kept up with growth.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Notes in the ledger

Chennai · structural

Chennai

Chennai is a split-admin metro: civic services run by the Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), law-and-order by a Police Commissioner (not a district SP). GCC's 2025-26 budget projects ₹5,145 cr revenue against ₹5,214 cr expenditure — a small planned deficit. Property tax + stamp duty dominate own revenue (property tax alone >₹2,000 cr).

Built where water returns

Chennai · 2025

Pallikaranai marsh (Ramsar site)

NGT froze ALL building approvals inside the Ramsar boundary + 1-km influence zone; CMDA implemented via Office Order 07/2025 (9 Oct 2025). DGPS survey: 38% of the marsh occupied (GCC 173.56 ha, ELCOT 163.25 ha, Railways 46.92 ha, IT park 5.85 ha).

Chennai · 2024

Pallikaranai marsh

NGT directed eviction of illegal structures to retrieve marsh; Forest Dept + GCC demolished 70 encroaching houses (Mahalakshmi Nagar) Nov 2024; families resettled via TNUHDB.
