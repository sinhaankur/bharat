These are ESTIMATES from a single popular-history compilation. White himself stresses that pre-modern tolls are guesses drawn from a spread of historians, often with wide error bars. Figures are 'deaths in millions' as printed in the NYT graphic. We reproduce them faithfully and label the source; we do NOT present them as settled fact. Where a figure is especially contested, contested=true and a note explains why. India-relevant events are tagged india_region so the heritage atlas can link a temple's destruction to its regional toll.
