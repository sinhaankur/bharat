Built ~8th c. CE at the height of Karkota Kashmir; a masterwork blending Gandharan, Gupta and Graeco-Roman forms. Destroyed in the early 15th c.; today a protected ruin of 84 fluted columns on a plateau above the Kashmir Valley.
