The great pagan temple of Gamla Uppsala, described by Adam of Bremen as gold-adorned with idols of Thor, Odin and Freyr. Norse religion was suppressed as Sweden Christianised; the temple was destroyed/replaced by a church in the late 11th c. A royal burial-mound landscape survives.

    Medieval Christian conversion. The pagan cult site was suppressed and a church built at Gamla Uppsala as Norse religion was replaced by Christianity.
