Sources & references

Every number on the India fiscal map is sourced from public datasets — Finance Commission reports, RBI's Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, MoSPI advance estimates, Union Budget receipts, and the CMS-India Corruption Study. Headline percentages (Finance Commission horizontal shares, FY24 budget actuals) are exact. State-level breakdowns (GSDP, own tax, devolution per year) are approximations within ±10%, smoothed for year-on-year comparability. Each section below documents what we used and what we are deliberately approximating.

01Finance Commission devolution

Vertical pool + horizontal shares (exact figures)
  • Report of the 14th Finance Commission (2015–2020) FC-XIV · Government of India · 2014
    Recommended raising the vertical share of central taxes devolved to states from 32% to 42%. Horizontal formula weights: population (17.5%), demographic change (10%), area (15%), forest cover (7.5%), income distance (50%). PDF
  • Report of the 15th Finance Commission (2021–2026) FC-XV · Government of India · 2020 (final)
    Lowered vertical share to 41% (absorbed J&K/Ladakh becoming UTs). New horizontal formula weights: population 2011 (15%), area (15%), forest & ecology (10%), income distance (45%), demographic performance (12.5%), tax effort (2.5%). PDF
  • Union Budget — Receipts & Devolution Ministry of Finance · annual
    Actual vertical devolution figures FY15–FY24 (used as the annual pool ranges in the dashboard's vertical_pool_actuals). indiabudget.gov.in

02State GSDP & own tax revenue

Yearly data, current prices (approximated within ±10%)
  • RBI — Handbook of Statistics on Indian States Reserve Bank of India · annual
    Authoritative state-wise dataset for own tax revenue, debt-GSDP, fiscal deficit. Used for own-tax and devolution receipts cross-checks. RBI handbook
  • MoSPI — State-wise GSDP (current prices) Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation · advance + provisional estimates
    Primary source for GSDP series FY15–FY24. Recent years use first/second advance estimates and will be revised. mospi.gov.in
  • CAG — State Finance Audit Reports Comptroller & Auditor General · annual, by state
    Used to sanity-check devolution-received and grants-in-aid figures for major states. cag.gov.in

03Contribution to Center (estimated)

Heavily approximated — methodology disclosure
  • CBDT / DGTS — Income & Corporate Tax Collections Direct taxes by region · annual
    Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Delhi, Gujarat dominate direct-tax collections — used as anchors for the relative scaling. incometaxindia.gov.in
  • GST Council — State-wise SGST + IGST monthly GST collections
    SGST is destination-based, but IGST at origin gives a partial proxy for production-side contribution. State-wise monthly figures published since 2017. gstcouncil.gov.in
  • PRS Legislative Research — State Finance Notes PRS India · per-state annual briefs
    Used for triangulation of central-share receipts and grants. prsindia.org

04Governance footprint

IAS cadre strength, state employees, corruption perception
  • DoPT — Civil List, IAS Cadre Strength Department of Personnel & Training · annual civil list
    Approved (sanctioned) cadre strength per state. Important: 25–40% of cadre officers are typically on Central deputation at any time — the dashboard number is structural cap, not on-ground headcount. dopt.gov.in
  • Pay Research Unit — State Govt Employee Census Ministry of Finance / state finance reports
    State govt direct employee counts (in lakh). Excludes contract, outsourced, and PSU workers — which can add 30–60% more. doe.gov.in
  • CMS-India — India Corruption Study 2019 Centre for Media Studies · last edition before COVID
    % of households reporting they paid a bribe to access a public service in the past 12 months — most rigorous state-level corruption survey publicly available. CMS resumed sporadic studies post-COVID but full state coverage hasn't been replicated. cmsindia.org
  • State govt department lists Each state's secretariat website
    Departments listed in the detail card are illustrative of each state's structure — the "back-office" vs "public-facing" classification is editorial, drawn from published CAG audit reports and budget allocations.

05Geography — floods, coast, terrain & elevation

The physical-constraint layer (terrain, flood-prone, coastal CRZ, rivers, rainfall, elevation, change-over-time, encroachment) + four official-data risk layers: palaeochannels, unsafe/no-development zones, monsoon inundation, encroachment zones
  • MoEFCC — Coastal Regulation Zone Notification 2019 Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change
    Defines the CRZ that legally restricts near-shore construction. We flag the 73 districts that actually touch the sea; the CRZ category (I/II/III/IV) per district needs the district Coastal Zone Management Plan (CZMP) — a gap. moef.gov.in
  • CWC / NDMA / state DMAs — flood-prone areas Central Water Commission, NDMA, Rashtriya Barh Ayog, ASDMA/FMISC, ISRO-Bhuvan Flood Hazard Atlas
    15 chronically flood-prone states; 87 districts named as repeatedly affected are marked "district-chronic", others inherit the state flag (labelled). Annual flood-damage ₹ per district needs state relief memoranda — a gap. ndma.gov.in
  • IMD — district rainfall & India-WRIS — river basins India Meteorological Department; India Water Resources Information System
    State climate band + documented rainfall anchors (precise district annual-normal mm is on the IMD dynamic portal — a gap). River systems per state from India-WRIS. mausam.imd.gov.in · indiawris.gov.in
  • Open SRTM elevation (metres above sea level) NASA SRTM via open-elevation.com · per-point & per-district centroid
    Every district's centroid elevation, and the live hover "m above sea level" readout, come from open SRTM. A single representative point, not a within-district mean. open-elevation.com
  • 3D topography & hillshade — open 30 m DEM AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium terrain-RGB, SRTM/NASADEM); Esri World Hillshade; ISRO CartoDEM class
    The 3D relief, the map's hillshade overlay, the per-pixel elevation-tint overlay (blue lowland → white peaks, decoded live from Terrarium terrain-RGB), the map's Low-lying % facet (share of each district's — and each taluk's — area below 10 m / 5 m, polygon-masked raster; exposure proxy, not a flood model), the interactive flood explorer and the Kolkata flood simulation terrain are all built from open 30 m elevation. Honest gap: India has no open nationwide LIDAR — Indian LIDAR is project-specific/commercial and the 2021 Geospatial Guidelines restrict high-resolution data, so sub-metre LIDAR relief cannot be shown. 30 m open DEM (SRTM / ISRO CartoDEM on Bhuvan) is the honest best. AWS Terrain Tiles · Bhuvan CartoDEM
  • Sub-district boundaries — geoBoundaries ADM3 geoBoundaries gbOpen IND ADM3 · ODbL 1.0 · derived from the govt Local Government Directory
    6,800 sub-district (taluk/tehsil/block) polygons, simplified for the web, that let you zoom below the district. Source: lgdirectory.gov.in via Pathways Data. geoboundaries.org
  • Live rain radar & clouds — RainViewer RainViewer public API · global weather radar + infrared cloud, ~10-min updates · free/keyless
    The "Live weather" overlay shows real-time rain radar and clouds, refreshed every few minutes — so the map syncs to today's conditions (watch the monsoon flood Pune/Mumbai as it happens). Displayed under RainViewer attribution. rainviewer.com/api
  • Basemap imagery — Esri, OpenTopoMap, Copernicus Sentinel-2 Esri World Imagery; OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA); EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless (ESA Copernicus, open)
    The satellite / terrain / recent-satellite basemaps are displayed under each provider's attribution. Google Earth imagery is licensed and is not used or redistributed; Sentinel-2 is the open near-real-time equivalent. Deep zoom (to z21): over India, open imagery has real detail to about z19 (Esri) / z20 (CARTO); zooming further is an honest upscale of the last sharp tile (the map flags this and shows the scale) — no open, free, sub-metre basemap exists for India, and India's 2021 Geospatial Guidelines restrict high-resolution redistribution. An optional “Satellite HD” layer (real 50 cm, z21) can be enabled with your own Mapbox access token — it is opt-in, licensed, and no key is shipped in this repo.
  • Change-over-time & encroachment — per-case citations NGT / court / CAG / HYDRAA orders + peer-reviewed & major-press studies
    Water-body-loss timelines and encroachment cases (Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Srinagar, Delhi, Guwahati) each carry a specific source; 586 districts remain honest gaps rather than fabricated figures.
  • Palaeochannels — the river's natural old course ISRO-Bhuvan / NGDR palaeochannel mapping · CGWB · Geological Survey of India + press
    Where a settlement sits on a river's former bed, floods reclaim it ("you built on the old bed"). Pinned for Gurugram (Sahibi/Najafgarh), Delhi (Yamuna migration + Najafgarh drain), Saharsa (Kosi fan, 2008 avulsion), Kurukshetra (buried Saraswati/Ghaggar-Hakra) and Chennai (Adyar/Cooum + Pallikaranai). The exact channel polygon stays a gap. Bhuvan NGDR · CGWB · GSI
  • Official "unsafe for habitation" / no-development zones DDA Master Plan (Yamuna Zone-O) · CWC/NDMA flood-hazard · state SDMA declarations · GSI landslide susceptibility
    The official do-not-build signal — much stronger than our derived <100 m proxy. Pinned for Delhi (Yamuna Zone-O), Joshimath (Chamoli — declared unsafe/subsidence, 2023), Kedarnath valley (Rudra Prayag — flash-flood/GLOF hazard), Chennai (Pallikaranai + CRZ), Idukki (Western-Ghats landslide slopes). Per-district CZMP category (I/II/III/IV) stays a gap. CWC flood-hazard · DDA planning
  • Monsoon / seasonal inundation ISRO-Bhuvan flood-inundation (satellite seasonal extent) · IMD monsoon
    Which areas flood specifically in the monsoon — the recurring seasonal pattern behind the live rain radar. Pinned for Barpeta (Brahmaputra), Darbhanga (Kosi–Bagmati), Ballia (Ganga–Ghaghara), Kolkata (delta + tidal) and Puri (Mahanadi delta + cyclone). Precise seasonal area (ha) stays a gap. Bhuvan flood · IMD
  • Encroachment ZONES — the delineated boundary (not just the case) DDA Zone-O · Bombay-HC blue/red flood-lines (Pune) · lake FTL surveys (Hyderabad/Bengaluru) · district water-body registers (Gurugram)
    The actual encroached zone — floodplain / water-body / FTL / buffer line — where an authority has published the boundary. Pinned for Delhi (Zone-O), Pune (Mula-Mutha flood-lines), Hyderabad (lake FTL), Gurugram (640→251 water-body register) and Bengaluru (lake buffer + rajakaluve). The machine-readable GIS polygon geometry is almost always a gap — most cities lack open floodplain GIS.

07Block / taluk / tehsil (sub-district)

Sub-district polygons now ship for all India (see §05); names/population per block below.
  • geoBoundaries ADM3 — sub-district polygons (all India) geoBoundaries gbOpen IND ADM3 · ODbL 1.0 · from govt LGD
    Now shipped: 6,800 sub-district boundary polygons let you zoom below the district and hover a taluk/tehsil by name. Simplified for the web. Full source detail in §05 (Geography). geoboundaries.org
  • Census of India 2011 — Sub-District Directory Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner
    Block / taluk / tehsil names + populations. Not available as a single open CSV — locked in per-state PDF tables on the official portal. Block names hand-encoded from these directories cross-checked with state revenue dept sites; block populations still a gap. censusindia.gov.in
  • LGD (Local Government Directory) — Ministry of Panchayati Raj Official government source · POST-based, no open API
    Authoritative block / sub-district codes. Behind POST requests and stateful forms — requires per-state scraping with Selenium or undocumented endpoints. lgdirectory.gov.in
  • MGNREGA — block-level person-days & wage payments nrega.nic.in · monthly · not yet wired
    Largest centrally-sponsored scheme; reports at block level. No clean open API; data is hidden behind state→district→block dropdown forms. Wiring this gives a "rural employment delivery" view at block level. nrega.nic.in
  • Administrative head note structural fact, not data
    Each block is headed by a Tahsildar (revenue) and a Block Development Officer (development), both typically state civil service — not IAS. The IAS cadre stops at district HQ; only a tiny subset of younger IAS officers serve as Sub-Divisional Magistrates above the block, never AT the block.

Caveats & methodology

Per-state per-year figures are approximations within ±10% for GSDP, own tax, devolution, grants, and contribution. They are smoothed for year-on-year readability rather than reported to the exact rupee. Finance Commission horizontal shares are exact, sourced directly from the FC final reports.

Contribution to Center is necessarily an estimate. Most central tax incidence is destination-blind — GST is consumption-based, customs are collected at ports (not at origin of consumption), and corporate tax is collected at registered HQ (which concentrates in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru regardless of where activity happens). The dashboard uses a GSDP-weighted, formal-sector-share-adjusted estimate.

Corruption perception is from a 2019 survey — pre-COVID, pre-DBT-acceleration. Treat as directional, not as a 2024 number.

"Back-office" vs "public-facing" department classification is editorial. It draws on published CAG audit findings (which department's spending shows up most often in irregularity reports) and budget composition (salaries vs program spend ratios), but ultimately reflects an opinionated read.
