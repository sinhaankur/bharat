The house system

One chassis for every page of the atlas: lamp-dark ground, gold for what you can act on, a mono label for every source, and a dashed amber chip wherever the record runs out.

Grid

One grid per screen

Five screen types, one set of tokens. The bars show columns and gutters at true proportion within each screen type.

Phone0 to 599 px4 columns, 12 px gutter, 16 px marginContent: Full width
Tablet600 to 1023 px8 columns, 16 px gutter, 24 px marginContent: Full width
Laptop1024 to 1439 px12 columns, 20 px gutter, 32 px marginContent: 1240 px, centred
Desktop1440 to 1919 px12 columns, 24 px gutter, 40 px marginContent: 1240 px, centred
Wide1920 px and up12 columns, 28 px gutter, 48 px marginContent: 1360 px, centred

Each screen type sets four tokens, --cols, --gutter, --margin and --max. Header, footer and every page container sit on the same margins, so edges line up from page to page. Layouts use .grid with span classes: sp-4, sp-8 and the rest on laptop and up, sp-t4 and sp-t8 on tablet. On a phone every block takes the full row.

Motion

Subtle on the surface, deep underneath

Easing, everywhere

cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)

Micro
  • Reading-progress line
  • Cursor glow on cards and tiles
  • 0.97 press on buttons
  • Links underline by drawing in
  • Menu items stagger 18ms
  • Selected map point pings once
Deep, scroll-linked
  • Door emblem draws in behind the hero
  • Spine and story rails fill with light
  • Map areas fade in north to south
  • Accent word gets a gold underline sweep
  • Page change: 180ms out, 450ms in
  • All off under reduced motion

Rules

What the build must keep

01

Sourced, or it is a gap

No number is estimated to fill a hole. Gaps render as a dashed amber chip.

02

Fact and framing stay apart

Commentary is labelled: framing, commentary, the atlas’s own analysis.

03

Source tiers

T1 government PDF, T2 government web, T3 encyclopedia (discovery only, never linked), T4 news.

04

No emoji on branded surfaces

Icons come from the twelve Mauryan marks, inline.

05

Independent

Non-commercial, not affiliated with any government body: in every footer.

06

One name, one home

Each page has one name and one place in the six doors.
