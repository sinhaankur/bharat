How to read a pipeline. Left-to-right is the money's journey; colour is the appointing/owning level (red = Union, amber = State, green = local/elected, purple = outcome). The dashed chips below each pipeline are the gates — sanction, utilisation certificate, social audit, CAG. Red dashed gates are the ones that most often jam or get bypassed: a missing UC blocks the next tranche, a skipped social audit hides leakage, an unactioned CAG para is where accountability quietly dies.

Every figure that would fill these pipelines for a real district lives in district-ledger.json and is shown on a district pixel only when a government PDF backs it — otherwise it stays an honest gap. This page shows the structure; the map shows the amounts, where they exist.
