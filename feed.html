News, place by place

Every place gathers the news covering it — split by who's telling it (media lean, left→right) and who funds them (owner / funding type). Beside the coverage sits the reality: population, per-capita money, crime and prison figures — sourced or marked a gap, never invented.

History is written by the winner, and a "fact" can be disputable depending on who won. So we separate the two: the coverage is contested; the numbers underneath are not.
