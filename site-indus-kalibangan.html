On the dried Ghaggar-Hakra (identified by some with the Vedic Sarasvati), Kalibangan yielded rows of mud-brick 'fire altars' — among the few Harappan sites with them — argued to preforeshadow Vedic fire (yajna) ritual, and the earliest known ploughed field (c. 2800 BCE). The fire-altar interpretation is debated. ASI-excavated (B. B. Lal / B. K. Thapar).
