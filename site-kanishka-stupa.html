Kanishka Stupa (Shaji-ki-Dheri), Peshawar

A monumental relic stupa raised by Kanishka at Purushapura (Peshawar) — among the tallest buildings of the ancient world, described by Chinese pilgrims. The Kanishka casket was found here in 1908–09.
