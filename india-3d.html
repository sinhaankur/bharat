India in 3D — states & rivers

Every state raised in 3D, with the major rivers — the Ganga, Brahmaputra, Godavari, Cauvery, Chambal, Sutlej and more — drawn as their real courses. Rendered on India's full claimed boundary, including Pakistan-occupied Kashmir and Aksai Chin. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

The boundary is India's full claimed map. States use india-states.geojson, which extends to ~37° N and includes PoK (Gilgit-Baltistan) and Aksai Chin — the official Survey-of-India depiction, not the de-facto line-of-control. District-level geometry (Census-2011) stops short of the northern claimed area, so this states view is the correct one for the full boundary. Rivers are Natural Earth centre-lines (public domain), clipped to India — representative courses, not survey-grade channels.

States: india-states.geojson extruded by data from india-fiscal.json. Rivers: Natural Earth 10 m river centre-lines. Rendered client-side with three.js. For a true elevation surface see the 3D topography view; for states extruded by development constraint, the constraint atlas. This same map is reproducible offline in Blender via build_india_blender.py.
