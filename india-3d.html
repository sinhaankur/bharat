India on the globe — states, rivers & cities

India raised in 3D on the real Earth — a NASA-derived day/night globe with city lights and drifting clouds, in world context. Every state lifts off the sphere, the major rivers (Ganga, Brahmaputra, Godavari, Cauvery…) run as real courses, and the big cities are pinned. Rendered on India's full claimed boundary, including Pakistan-occupied Kashmir and Aksai Chin. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

The boundary is India's full claimed map. States use india-states.geojson, which extends to ~37° N and includes PoK (Gilgit-Baltistan) and Aksai Chin — the official Survey-of-India depiction, not the de-facto line-of-control. District-level geometry (Census-2011) stops short of the northern claimed area, so this states view is the correct one for the full boundary. Rivers are Natural Earth centre-lines (public domain), clipped to India — representative courses, not survey-grade channels.

States: india-states.geojson extruded by data from india-fiscal.json. Rivers: Natural Earth 10 m river centre-lines. Earth day / night / cloud textures: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0, derived from NASA Blue Marble / Visible Earth). World country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Rendered client-side with three.js. For a true elevation surface see the 3D topography view; for states extruded by development constraint, the constraint atlas. This same map is reproducible offline in Blender via build_india_blender.py.
