An 11th-century Paramara-era Sanskrit teaching hall and Saraswati temple built by Raja Bhoja; the presiding Vagdevi idol is now in the British Museum. The Kamal Maula mosque was raised using its parts. A 2024 ASI survey concluded the mosque structure was made from an earlier Paramara-period temple. A live, court-contested site (Places of Worship Act, 1991).
