A giant sandstone monolith sacred to the Anangu, woven through their Tjukurpa (creation law) — among the world's oldest continuous sacred sites, holy for tens of thousands of years. Climbing was permanently banned in 2019 out of respect. A living Aboriginal holy place. UNESCO World Heritage.
