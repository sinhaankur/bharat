History's 100 deadliest events — wars, oppressions, failed states, despots, colonial and civil wars — sized by the number of people killed and, where the span was under 40 years, by the share of all humanity that died. This is an interactive rebuild of Bill Marsh's 2011 New York Times graphic, which visualised the death-toll estimates catalogued by Matthew White. Events that happened in or heavily struck the Indian subcontinent are outlined in gold.
