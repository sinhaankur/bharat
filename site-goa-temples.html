The temples of the Goan coastal talukas were systematically demolished after the Portuguese conquest (1510) and during the Goa Inquisition (1560–1812); many principal deities were carried inland and re-enshrined (e.g. Saptakoteshwara at Narve, Mangeshi, Shantadurga). Included here so the record of temple destruction is not attributed to a single actor.

What happened to it

  1. 1560 CEPortuguese colonial authorities & the Goa Inquisition

    Portuguese Estado da Índia / Catholic Inquisition. Portuguese decrees ordered the demolition of Hindu temples in the Old Conquests; the Inquisition (1560–1812) prosecuted, tortured and executed converts and non-Christians. Estimates of temples destroyed run into the hundreds.

    estimate Thousands tried by the Inquisition; hundreds executed (auto-da-fé). A documented non-Islamic destruction of Dharmic sites.
