Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, Ujjain

On the Shipra at Ujjain, the only south-facing (dakshinamukhi) Jyotirlinga, famed for the daily Bhasma Aarti. Damaged under Iltutmish (1235) and later rebuilt by the Marathas. A major living temple.
