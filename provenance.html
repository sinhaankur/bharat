How to read this

Level matters. Many dimensions (language, politics, geography, housing, vehicles, aviation) carry a real source but show a state‑proxy or state‑official figure while the per‑district number is a declared gap — because no official per‑district series exists yet. That is honestly a gap, not a fabrication, and it's counted as one here.

Regenerate any time with python3 gen_provenance.py. It re-walks the ledger and the safety layer, so the counts below always reflect the shipped data.
