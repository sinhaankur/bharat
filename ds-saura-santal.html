Saura / Santal

the icon-mural, and a script made on purpose

Before: Saura idital wall-icons honoured ancestors in white on red-ochre — and the Munda languages had no script of their own. After: Raghunath Murmu devised Ol Chiki for Santali in 1925; it is now official and in Unicode — the art's missing alphabet, finally written.

Design idea: a whole mural as one framed icon, figures woven into a border like a single glyph. The real lesson: a design system can include inventing the writing itself.
