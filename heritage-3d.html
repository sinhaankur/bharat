A few sites have a curated model (Angkor Wat, Borobudur); for the rest we search Sketchfab by name — which surfaces community photogrammetry of many ruins too (Martand, Hampi, Nalanda). Where a site was destroyed and nothing survives to scan, the viewer says so plainly — that absence is the point. Pair this with the map & records on the heritage atlas.
