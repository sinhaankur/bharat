A complex of seven temples around a holy pool said in legend to have formed from Shiva's tears; linked to the Pandavas' exile (Dvaitavana). A major pilgrimage site abandoned at the 1947 Partition; partly restored, worship resumed for pilgrim groups.

Recorded damage

What happened to it

  1. 1947 CEPartition of India (abandonment & neglect)

    1947 Partition. The Hindu community migrated to East Punjab in 1947 and the complex was abandoned; decades of neglect (and groundwater loss to nearby cement plants) damaged it. Not a deliberate demolition — a loss through displacement.

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
