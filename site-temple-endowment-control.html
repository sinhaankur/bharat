The East India Company / Crown brought major temples under state financial control — the Madras Regulation VII of 1817 and the Religious Endowments Act 1863 — administering (and drawing revenue from) temple endowments. Post-independence, this became the state Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) departments, under which many large temples' finances are still government-administered — a live debate about the state's control of Dharmic institutions.
