A colossal three-storeyed Shiva temple — reportedly 1,600 pillars, 12 gateways and 11 Rudra shrines. Dismantled in 1296 by Alauddin Khilji's generals, then in 1414–15 its western part was converted into the Jami Masjid by Ahmed Shah I of Gujarat. Only two toranas and four pillars of the central structure survive; an ASI Monument of National Importance.
