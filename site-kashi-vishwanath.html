Varanasi's principal Shiva shrine. The 16th-c. temple was demolished in 1669 under Aurangzeb and the Gyanvapi mosque raised on the site; the present temple was built adjacent by Ahilyabai Holkar in 1780.

What happened to it

  1. 1669 CEAurangzeb

    Mughal Emperor. The Maasir-i-Alamgiri (official court history) records the 1669 order to demolish the temple. The Gyanvapi mosque stands on the site.
