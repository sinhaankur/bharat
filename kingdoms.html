The standards that outlasted empires

The princely states kept their own flags through the Mughal centuries and under British paramountcy, until they joined India in 1947–1950. Each standard is drawn from a written description; where the source names the colours but not their shade, the shade is representative.

How long each one lasted

Every bar is woven in its flag's colours, from founding to the year it joined India or fell. Most kept their thrones through the Mughal centuries and under British paramountcy, until 1947 to 1950.
