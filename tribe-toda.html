Pigments as tokens

Each swatch is a material with a name. Contrast is measured against the ground, so you can see which pigments can carry text and which stay in the pattern.

unbleached white cotton #efeadc · GROUND — on itself
red wool #b3261e · DISPLAY · ACCENT 5.4:1 on ground · AA
black wool #191512 · TEXT 15.1:1 on ground · AAA
blue (occasional) #2d4f8a · PATTERN 6.7:1 on ground · AA

GRAMMAR

Design idea: a grid you can count. Red means youth and vitality, black wisdom and maturity; motifs are geometric only, never curved or floral.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: women of the Toda, a pastoral people and one of Tamil Nadu’s PVTGs, embroider the putukuli shawl by counting warp and weft, so the work looks woven and both sides are usable. After: Geographical Indication in March 2013, held jointly by Pompuhar, Keystone Foundation and the Toda Nalavaazhvu Sangam; machine copies are an infringement.
