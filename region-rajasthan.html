Rajasthan’s twelve

Twelve tribes are listed, but two carry almost the whole count: the Mina and the Bhil. They live mainly in the south and southeast, in districts that were once the princely states of Banswara, Dungarpur and Mewar’s hills.

The most populous tribe in the state.

OLDEST

Second largest; the Bhil and Garasia followed Govind Guru at Mangarh.

Saharia

SOUTH

Garasia and Damor

With the Dhanka and Saharia, about 6.6% of the ST count in 2001.

Kingdoms

Tribes under the princely states

Mangarh sat on the border of Banswara and Sunth, and the troops sent there included the Mewar Bhil Corps. The kingdoms of Rajputana, from Jodhpur to Mewar, each had their own flag and colours; they get their own page next.

When

The time periods

1913

Mangarh

On 17 November 1913 troops fired on a Bhil assembly led by Govind Guru on Mangarh hill, on the border of the princely states of Banswara and Sunth. The Prime Minister put the dead at more than 1,500.

1950

The first list

The Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950 is notified on 6 September 1950. A tribe’s status is valid only in the state that lists it.
