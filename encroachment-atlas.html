Built where the water returns

Where construction sits on land the water reclaims — floodplains, lakebeds and wetlands. Every case here has been ruled on by the National Green Tribunal, a court, or flagged by CAG. This is why the ground floods: it was always water.

The floodplain doesn't forget. A lakebed filled for real estate, a riverfront reclaimed, a marsh built over — the water comes back to where it always was.
