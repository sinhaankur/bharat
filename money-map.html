5,180sq km in the Shaksgam Valley, ceded by Pakistan to China in 1963

India's official map includes the whole of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. Parts of both are administered by Pakistan and by China. The Government of India has told Parliament repeatedly that these areas are Indian territory under illegal occupation, and that it has never recognised the 1963 China-Pakistan boundary agreement. Pakistan and China dispute this: Pakistan claims the former princely state, and China claims Aksai Chin. The hatched areas on every map in this atlas mark them; select one to read the record.

Figures and position: Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1740, 27 July 2016 T1 and Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 2793, 11 August 2010 T1. Outlines are from Natural Earth and are approximate; they are not survey lines.

On "corruption": the atlas holds no corruption score and does not make one. What it holds are documents: delays, audits and freezes on record, issues cited against an office, and notes in the ledger. Those counts are deepest where research has gone deepest (West Bengal, Bihar), so a dark state may simply be an unresearched one. Units: ₹ thousand crore (₹ '000 cr) at current prices, FY24. Per-state figures are approximations within ±10% for headline metrics; FC shares are exact. 'Contribution' is an estimated federal-tax footprint (income tax, corporate tax, GST/IGST origin, customs) — methodology is necessarily approximate because most central tax incidence is destination-blind. Sources: Finance Commission XIV & XV reports (horizontal shares); RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States (GSDP, own tax); Union Budget Receipts (vertical devolution actuals); MoSPI advance estimates (recent GSDP).
