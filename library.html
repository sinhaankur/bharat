An insider MEMOIR, not a history. Administrative, political and personal content run in near-equal measure (~8–9 per 1,000 words each), but the first-person voice is far denser (16.9/1k) — this is one man's vantage, and the personal/gossip register is as heavy as the politics. Read as testimony, weighed, not as neutral record.
