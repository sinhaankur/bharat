Bathtub model, not a flood forecast. Every cell below the water level counts as "wet" — real floods depend on rainfall, river flow, drainage, pumping and embankments, none of which are modelled here. It answers one honest question: how much of this district's ground sits below a given water height? Same source and method as the map's Low-lying % layer. For a full fluid-dynamics simulation on Kolkata's terrain, see the flood simulation ↗.

Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM-derived, open, ~30 m) decoded in your browser — nothing stored or redistributed. District boundaries: repo geojson (Census 2011 — Telangana isn't listed separately; undivided Andhra Pradesh covers it). Terrain colour = metres above sea level; grey = outside the district.
