Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Gurugram = NCR financial-services and IT hub; auto manufacturing in Manesar/Faridabad.
  • Highest per-capita income among large states (~₹3.0 lakh in FY24).
  • Strong own-tax effort — fiscal capacity matches its small size.

Strains on record

  • FC horizontal share at ~1.1% is dwarfed by what it sends as contribution.
  • Severe net donor: ~5–6× contribution vs devolution.
  • Air pollution + Yamuna depletion; NCR environmental costs externalised.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Built where water returns

Gurgaon · 2023

Ghata / Basai / Najafgarh jheel + Badshahpur drain

A Gurugram admin study to the NGT found 389 water bodies lost since 1956 (640→251); Ghata lake shrank ~370 ac → ~2 ac; Basai wetland ~25% of original by 2022; Badshahpur storm-drain encroached. INTACH moved the SC (Jul 2023) over the Najafgarh embankment; matter before the NGT.
