GRAMMAR

Design idea: horror vacui as a layout rule — no empty space, a double outline on every shape, and bold fields of saturated natural dye.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: women of Mithila painted kohbar wedding-chamber walls for centuries, filling every space with nature and deity. After: the 1934 drought and later paper commissions moved it to handmade sheets; now one of India's most recognised folk forms.
