Each swatch is a material with a name. Contrast is measured against the ground, so you can see which pigments can carry text and which stay in the pattern.

GRAMMAR

Design idea: sgraffito as a system: one dark token, one light token, and a single subtractive tool. Line weight comes from the comb’s teeth, not a brush.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: for the marriage chamber, the wall is coated in black earth, then white kaolin; while the white is still wet, it is etched away with a datwan twig, a comb or fingers, so the black shows through. After: registered with Sohrai as one Geographical Indication in 2020.
