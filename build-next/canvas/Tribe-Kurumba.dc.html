GRAMMAR

Design idea: a palette harvested, not mixed: each token has a single plant or soil behind it, and blue is absent because the hills do not give it.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: after worship of the guardian deity, painters collect colour from the forest: coconut shells under the vengai tree catch a sap that gives yellow, brown and black. Fewer than ten practitioners remain. After: a posthumous Padma Shri for the painter Krishnan “Kitna” Raghavan drew national attention to the form.
