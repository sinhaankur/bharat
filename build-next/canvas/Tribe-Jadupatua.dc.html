GRAMMAR

Design idea: sequence as layout. Each panel leads to the next, figures are flat with no perspective, and the colour count stays small on purpose.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: vertical scrolls of paper sheets sewn together, edged with cloth and mounted on bamboo rods, painted with goat-hair brushes, then unrolled panel by panel while the painter sings. After: the related Paitkar scrolls of Amadubi survive mostly as small tourist pieces.
