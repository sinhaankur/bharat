Rules

What the build must keep

01

Sourced, or it is a gap

No number is estimated to fill a hole. Gaps render as a dashed amber chip.

02

Fact and framing stay apart

Commentary is labelled: framing, commentary, the atlas’s own analysis.

03

Source tiers

T1 government PDF, T2 government web, T3 encyclopedia (discovery only, never linked), T4 news.

04

No emoji on branded surfaces

Icons come from the twelve Mauryan marks, inline.

05

Independent

Non-commercial, not affiliated with any government body: in every footer.

06

One name, one home

Each page has one name and one place in the six doors.
