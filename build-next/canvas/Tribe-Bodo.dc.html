GRAMMAR

Design idea: the agor motif row. Small geometric units, from flowers, birds and mountains, repeat along a narrow band in bright pairs.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: Bodo women weave the aronai on handlooms and dye the yarn themselves: red from mangosteen and sapphire berry, orange from yellow mangosteen and coral jasmine. It is worn by men and women, and given to guests and loved ones. After: Geographical Indication in October 2024, one of 21 for Bodoland’s products.
