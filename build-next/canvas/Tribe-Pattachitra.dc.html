GRAMMAR

Design idea: a strict iconographic grammar — ornate borders, flat figures, no shadow. Everything is outline and fill, like a hand-inked icon set.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: temple-town painters made pattachitra — cloth scrolls of Jagannath and the epics — in mineral and shell pigments, bound to the Puri temple's ritual calendar. After: Raghurajpur became a living heritage craft village; the form now travels as fine art.
