What happened to it

  1. 1026 CEMahmud of Ghazni

    Ghaznavid Sultan. Sacked and looted the temple; contemporary and later Persian chronicles (e.g. later accounts drawing on al-Biruni's era) claim tens of thousands killed defending it and the idol broken.

    estimate Chronicles claim ~50,000 defenders killed; figure is a medieval estimate, not verified.

  2. 1299 CEAlauddin Khilji's forces (Ulugh Khan)

    Delhi Sultanate

  3. 1395 CEMuzaffar Shah I of Gujarat

    Gujarat Sultanate

  4. 1706 CEAurangzeb

    Mughal Emperor

Accounts and tolls from medieval chronicles are estimates with wide spread among historians. The atlas records damage by every actor on record.
