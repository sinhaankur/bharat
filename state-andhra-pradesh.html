Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • Long coastline (~970 km) — ports (Visakhapatnam, Krishnapatnam) anchor logistics.
  • Pharma cluster in Visakhapatnam; second-largest in India after Hyderabad.
  • Strong own-tax effort — agricultural tax administration relatively effective.

Strains on record

  • Lost Hyderabad to Telangana in 2014 bifurcation — capital city construction at Amaravati stalled, then restarted.
  • Special-category status promised in 2014 but never granted — perennial center-state grievance.
  • FC15 cut horizontal share slightly despite bifurcation hardship narrative.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.

Built where water returns

Hyderabad · 2024

Hussain Sagar + city lakes/nalas

HYDRAA razed encroachments within lake FTL/buffer from Aug 2024 (Khanapur, Gandipet, Shankerpally); GHMC show-cause to Pradeep Constructions for a 17-storey tower found inside Hussain Sagar FTL (2026). >30,000 nala encroachments identified.

Krishna · 2024

Budameru rivulet / Kolleru–Upputeru system

Lakhs settled on Budameru flood banks/plains (2011–2024), cutting capacity; Kolleru lake + the Upputeru outlet to the sea also encroached. After the 2024 deluge (35 dead) the state launched Operation Budameru to clear encroachments — but still lacks comprehensive encroachment data.
