Each census counted tribal people differently, so these figures are shown side by side, not as one line. The 1941 count is for undivided India, before the borders of 1947.

1941 · Undivided India 2.54 crore 25,441,489 of 383,643,745 people (about 6.6%) were counted as “Tribal”. A community or religion category of the 1941 Census (“tribal religion”), not today’s Scheduled Tribe list, so it is not directly comparable. T4B. R. Ambedkar, Pakistan or the Partition of India, Appendix I, from the 1941 Census community tables
1951 · India gap The first count under the new Scheduled Tribe orders. The national figure is published in the 1951 Census and in a 2001 Rajya Sabha answer; we have not yet extracted it. T1Rajya Sabha, unstarred question 3106, 19 December 2001 (statements for 1951 to 1991)
1961 · India 6.9% Scheduled Tribes were 6.9% of the population. Lists changed between censuses as tribes were added or removed, so shares move partly for that reason. T4FAO paper on the 1991 Census, citing Census of India figures
2011 · India 10.42 crore 10.42 crore people, 8.6% of the population and 11.3% of the rural population. Lists are made state by state: a community listed in one state need not be listed in another (Vikaspedia, Government of India).
