Sacred ground of the world
who built it — and who destroyed it

A geolocated atlas of the world's sacred sites — Sanatan Dharma, Buddhist, Jain, Sikh, Adivasi & indigenous, ancient native faiths (Egyptian, Greek, Norse, Mesoamerican, Andean, African, Zoroastrian, Yazidi), Christian, pre-Vedic megalithic and Indus Valley grounds — across all six inhabited continents. For each: who built it, when, and its lifespan — built, damaged or destroyed, and what stands today. Where a site fell, we name the actor and cite the record.

A temple is not only stone. Its lifespan is a ledger: who raised it, what it meant, and — where it fell — who brought it down and at what human cost. Sourced from the ASI and scholarship, or marked a gap. Claims not accepted by archaeology are shown separately and labelled contested.
