Sacred ground of Bharat — who built it, and who destroyed it

A geolocated atlas of Sanatan Dharma, Jain, Buddhist and pre-Vedic megalithic sites across Bharat, the Silk Route and Southeast Asia. For each: who built it (the king, dynasty or people), when, and its lifespan — built, damaged or destroyed, and what stands today. Where a site was destroyed, we name the actor and cite the record.

A temple is not only stone. Its lifespan is a ledger: who raised it, what it meant, and — where it fell — who brought it down and at what human cost. Sourced from the ASI and scholarship, or marked a gap. Claims not accepted by archaeology are shown separately and labelled contested.
