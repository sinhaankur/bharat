Traditionally the first Buddhist temple in China (68 CE), founded when two Indian monks arrived carrying sutras on a white horse — the literal beginning of Buddhism's transmission into China. Still active; now uniquely hosts Chinese, Indian, Thai and Burmese halls side by side.
