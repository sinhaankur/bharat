Brand

Peacock teal is the primary voice (headlines, links, brand). Terracotta clay is the single accent (calls-to-action, emphasis, live). Colour always carries meaning — never decoration.

Color tokens

Reference these CSS variables — never hard-code hex. The whole site re-skins from this palette. Light and dark both stay warm (bone paper / teal-charcoal night).

Type scale

Display serif Fraunces for headlines, clean sans Geist for body, mono Geist Mono for labels & data. One scale (~1.2 ratio) — use the tokens, not ad-hoc px.

Spacing

A 4px-based rhythm. --sp-1 … --sp-12. Consistent gaps = calm layouts.

Radius

Soft, friendly corners. --radius-sm/‑/‑lg/‑full.

Elevation

One shadow ladder. Cards rest at --shadow-xs, lift to --shadow-lg on hover.

UX principles

The rules behind the pixels. Full detail in how it works and DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md.

  1. Lead with the reader's question. The headline is the thing they'd Google.
  2. One idea per section. Eyebrow names it, H2 states it, lede answers it.
  3. Show, then tell. Map/chart first, prose second.
  4. Card-based discovery. Doorways, not walls of links.
  5. Progressive depth. Skimmable top → detail on demand.
  6. Sourced, or it's a gap. Never fabricated; the honesty shows in the UI.
  7. Colour carries meaning. Teal = us/navigation, terracotta = action/alert.
  8. Honour reduce-motion & focus. Every animation degrades; every control has a focus ring.
