Privacy & site policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

India Fiscal Map is an independent civic-data project. This page explains what data the site handles, how advertising and cookies work, and our editorial & bias policy. Plain language, no surprises.

1. What we collect

The site itself is static and does not run its own accounts, logins, or server-side database. We do not ask for or store personal information directly. If analytics or advertising are enabled (see below), those third parties may collect data per their own policies.

2. Cookies & advertising

If and when advertising is enabled via Google AdSense:

  • Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visit history. Users may opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Third-party vendors' use of cookies is governed by Google's advertising policies.
  • EU/UK/India users may be shown a consent notice where required.

Status: advertising is not currently active on this site. This section is in place for when it is.

3. News aggregation & outside links

The news layer links to third-party news outlets and shows only a headline, a short snippet, the outlet name, and a link. We do not republish full articles. Clicking a source takes you to that outlet, which has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for third-party content.

4. Editorial, sourcing & bias policy

  • Sourced or it's a gap. Every figure carries a government source + tier; an unsourced number stays blank and is marked a gap — never estimated.
  • Bias labels are aggregation, not our verdict. Outlet lean ratings come from published third-party media-bias assessments, shown to compare coverage — we do not assign our own political ratings.
  • Juxtaposition, not accusation. Where political alignment or "scale of problem" is shown, it sits beside the facts for context; we do not assert causation or compute an outrage/bias score.
  • Independent. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government body. See methodology & disclaimer.

5. Corrections & contact

Found an error or want something corrected/removed? Open an issue on the public repository: github.com/sinhaankur/india-fiscal-map/issues. Accuracy matters more than being first.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as the site evolves; the "last updated" date above will change.
