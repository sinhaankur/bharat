GRAMMAR

Design idea: stripe widths as the scale. Two inches of red, an inch of white, thin yellow lines between green: a spacing system written in yarn.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: the Pawndum is woven in two loom widths, black weft with bold red warp stripes dyed from plant bark; given as a dowry it is a zawlpuan. The Puanchei sets red stripes against white bands carrying named motifs. After: five Mizo puans received Geographical Indications in August 2019, applied for by the state since 2015.
