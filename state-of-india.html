State of India — who carries the country

India is one union of very unequal states. This is the whole country at a glance: every state's weight and impact — its people, its economy, and the real one — its net contribution to the union (what it puts into the central tax pool minus what flows back as devolution + grants). A few states carry the many. Switch the metric; click any state to open it, or hover a row and tap ⤴ to share that state's stat.

What "net contribution" means — and its honest limits. It's each state's contribution to the central tax pool minus what it gets back as devolution + grants (latest year, from india-fiscal.json). Positive = the state gives the union more than it receives; negative = it's net-supported. This is a real and important lens on impact — but it is not a verdict on a state's worth: poorer states receive more by design (that's what a union is for), and contribution tracks where economic activity and company HQs sit, not only where value is created. State-level figures are approximations within ~±10% (see sources), smoothed for comparability.

Data: india-fiscal.json (GSDP, own-tax, devolution, grants, contribution per state — RBI / Finance Commission / budget-derived). See the money flow live on the map, raised on the 3D globe, and audited in the provenance ledger. Sourced, or it's a gap — never faked.
