Sensō-ji, Asakusa (Tokyo)

Tokyo's oldest temple, dedicated to Kannon — the East-Asian form of the bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. Destroyed in WWII bombing and rebuilt; one of the most-visited religious sites in the world.
