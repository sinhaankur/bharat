Commentary, labelled

Two sides of the ledger

Strengths on record

  • First fully organic state in India (2016) — premium agri-export brand.
  • Highest per-capita GSDP among NE states; pharma cluster pre-2024.
  • Cleanest, lowest-corruption rankings consistently.

Strains on record

  • Tiny population means absolute fiscal numbers are small; concentration risk.
  • GSDP heavily dependent on pharma units that may relocate post tax-holiday expiry.
  • 2023 Teesta-III dam disaster + repeated GLOFs are climate-financial risk.

These summaries are the atlas's own analysis, kept apart from the sourced figures above.
