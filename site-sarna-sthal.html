Sarnaism is the indigenous nature-religion of the Chhotanagpur plateau — worship under the sal tree in a sacred grove (sarna sthal), no idol, no building. Millions of Adivasis follow it; there is a long-standing demand for a separate 'Sarna' religion code in the Indian census. Marked as a tradition-wide entry (representative point at Ranchi), because it is a living faith of tens of millions that most maps simply omit.
