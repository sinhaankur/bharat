Ancient India, on one spine

Five thousand years, read five ways at once. For each era — from the deep prehistoric substrate through the Indus cities, the Vedic age, the Mahajanapadas, Ashoka's empire and the Gupta classical age — see the language spoken, the script it was written in, the people (from ancient DNA), the rulers, and the heritage they left. Every era is a doorway into a deeper page.

This is the connective tissue: language, script, blood, power and stone, threaded into one story. Established facts and contested interpretations are kept apart — the ‘Aryan migration’ point especially.
