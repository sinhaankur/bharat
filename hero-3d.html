Raising the land…

One land,
in layers.

Scroll to fall through India — and watch it come apart into the strata an atlas is made of.

Descend

Layer 01 · the political

Thirty-six pieces lift apart.

Twenty-eight states, eight union territories — each rising by what constrains it on the ground: coast to protect, floods to survive, terrain too hard to build on.

Layer 02 · the hydrological

Rivers float above the land.

The Ganga, the Brahmaputra, the Godavari, the Cauvery — lifted off as their own stratum. They settled every city and still move most of the country's water, money and silt.

Layer 03 · the risk surface

Then it burns into colour.

Vermilion where coast and floodplain overlap. Bronze and granite for plateau, plains, desert. Every hue is a documented constraint — the CRZ, the CWC flood maps, the terrain index — never a verdict.

Layer 04 · and underneath, the data

Seven hundred districts,
each with a ledger.

Every point of light is a district you can open — money, land, people, risk, sourced and per-district.
