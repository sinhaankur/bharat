Photoreal 3D Earth

A true photorealistic 3D Earth — real 3D buildings and terrain, worldwide, country by country — using Google Maps Platform Photorealistic 3D Tiles rendered with CesiumJS. This is the Google-Earth-grade view. Because it streams Google's data, it needs your own Google API key — we ship no key, and it never leaves your browser.

🌍 Bring your own Google key

Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles are a paid Google service (free tier, then billed). Enable the Map Tiles API in Google Maps Platform, create an API key, and paste it below. It's stored only in this browser (localStorage) and is never sent to us or committed.

How to get a key + enable the Map Tiles API ↗
This is Google's data, not ours. The photoreal 3D buildings and terrain are streamed live from Google Maps Platform under your key and their Terms of Service — usage is billed to your Google account, and Google's copyright/attribution shows on the map. Nothing here is stored or redistributed. For the atlas's own free, open, sourced-or-gap globe (no key needed), use India in 3D.

Renderer: CesiumJS (Apache-2.0). Tiles: Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles (your key). To reset your key, clear this site's localStorage.
