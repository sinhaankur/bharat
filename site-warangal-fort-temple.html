The great Svayambhu temple at the heart of the Kakatiya capital Warangal, famed for its ornate carved toranas (the Kakatiya Kala Thoranam). Destroyed when the Delhi Sultanate annexed Warangal (1323); the shattered gateways and platform survive within the fort. On India's UNESCO tentative list.
